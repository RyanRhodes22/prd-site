Why general contractors call us back

Sitework goes wrong in the gaps between scopes. We close them before the bid goes out.

Quantities you can level

Every bid reconciles line-for-line against the engineer's quantity table. When our number differs from the plans, we say why on the face of the proposal instead of burying it.

We read the soils report

Compaction, scarify depth, moisture, over-excavation, reuse of existing material — priced to the geotech, not to a rule of thumb. Changed conditions are classified up front, not discovered in the field.

Clean scope boundaries

Inclusions and exclusions written so nobody has to guess who carries what. That is the difference between a change order priced before it happens and an argument after.
